## About the course

This WSQ courseware package develops organisation-level greenhouse-gas accounting, reporting, risk management, reduction planning, renewable-energy evaluation and carbon-offset strategy aligned with ISO 14064-1:2018 and the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Innovate organisational GHG accounting systems using current climate science, ISO 14064-1 principles and broader sustainability initiatives.
- LO2: Develop risk-informed GHG management plans that meet relevant reporting and regulatory requirements and support mitigation decisions.
- LO3: Develop operational emissions-reduction strategies across product lifecycles, value chains, supply chains and renewable-energy options.
- LO4: Generate economically viable carbon-offset and carbon-credit strategies aligned with organisational targets and residual-emissions needs.

## Topics covered

1. Foundations of Organisational GHG Accounting Systems
2. Regulatory-Compliant and Risk-Responsive GHG Management
3. Operationalising Emissions Reduction Across Value Chains
4. Designing Viable Carbon Offset and Strategy Plans
