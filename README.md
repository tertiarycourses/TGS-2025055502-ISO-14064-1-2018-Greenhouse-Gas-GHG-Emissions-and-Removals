# ISO 14064-1:2018 Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions and Removals

Course code: **TGS-2025055502**  
Release: **v2.0 — 20 August 2026**

This WSQ courseware package develops organisation-level greenhouse-gas accounting, reporting, risk management, reduction planning, renewable-energy evaluation and carbon-offset strategy through the connected **EcoForge Asia** case study.

## Courseware

- [Public courseware folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1MZbPOhfPc1P1slblFaC73maPI9yMBMC-)
- Trainer slide deck in editable PowerPoint and PDF formats
- Detailed Learner Guide in Word, PDF and Markdown formats
- Two-day Lesson Plan in Word and PDF formats
- Ten self-contained activity folders with instructions, printable packs and Excel workbooks

## Activities

1. Set the GHG Inventory Purpose and Boundaries
2. Build a Significant GHG Source and Sink Register
3. Quantify a Base-Year Inventory and Reporting Guide
4. Review a GHG Inventory and Resolve Quality Findings
5. Build a GHG Governance, Compliance and Risk Plan
6. Diagnose Hotspots and Set a Reduction Baseline
7. Design a Supplier and Product Reduction Roadmap
8. Develop a Renewable-Energy Business Case
9. Evaluate Carbon Offset Projects and Economics
10. Integrate the Organisational Carbon Strategy

Assessment papers and answer keys are controlled separately and are not published in this repository.
