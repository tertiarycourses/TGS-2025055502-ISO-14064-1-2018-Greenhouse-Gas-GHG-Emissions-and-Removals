# Activity 7 — Design a Supplier and Product Reduction Roadmap

**Alignment:** K3 · K9 · A1 · LO3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Objective:** Develop quantified value-chain actions, supplier engagement, product levers and monitoring milestones.

## Scenario

Top suppliers provide mixed data: one aluminium supplier has a verified product footprint, two provide facility averages, and several only provide spend and weight. Product engineers can redesign casing mass and fan efficiency, Logistics can change mode and load factor, and Procurement can include emissions criteria at renewal.

## Materials
- Supplier segmentation data
- Product and logistics options
- Illustrative cost and abatement estimates
- Roadmap workbook

## Questions
- Which suppliers warrant deep engagement first?
- How will data improve from estimates to supplier-specific evidence?
- Which product and logistics actions deliver credible reductions?
- How will progress and rebound effects be monitored?

## Detailed procedure
1. Segment suppliers by emissions, strategic importance, influence, data maturity and transition readiness.
2. Define a supplier-data ladder from spend-based estimates to verified product or supplier-specific data.
3. Design engagement routes: request, educate, collaborate, contract, incentivise and escalate.
4. Quantify product-material, efficiency, freight-mode and load-factor opportunities using transparent assumptions.
5. Check each option for quality, safety, cost, lifecycle burden shift and double counting.
6. Prioritise initiatives using abatement, feasibility, marginal cost, lead time and strategic value.
7. Create 30-, 90-, 180- and 720-day milestones with owners, dependencies and decision gates.
8. Define leading and lagging indicators, including supplier response, primary-data coverage and realised tCO2e reduction.
9. Conduct a red-team review of one claim and one forecast before roadmap approval.

## Required evidence
- Supplier segmentation and data ladder
- Quantified initiative cards
- 24-month roadmap
- KPI and governance plan

## Acceptance check

The roadmap contains quantified, owned and time-bound actions; supplier data improves progressively; product actions address lifecycle trade-offs and claim boundaries.
