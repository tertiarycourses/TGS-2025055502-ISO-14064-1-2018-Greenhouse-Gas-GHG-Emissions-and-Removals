# Activity 10 — Integrate the Organisational Carbon Strategy

**Alignment:** K2 · K7 · K8 · K9 · A2 · A5 · LO4  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Objective:** Combine inventory governance, reduction pathways, renewable energy and high-integrity offsets into an executable strategy.

## Scenario

EcoForge expects production to grow 35% by 2030. The board will fund projects only if cash flow and operational resilience remain acceptable. Customers demand supplier-specific reductions, the lender tests performance annually, and Marketing wants a simple climate claim. The strategy must show gross emissions, reductions, residuals and any credit use transparently.

## Materials
- Outputs from Activities 1-9
- 2030 growth and budget assumptions
- Initiative cost/abatement portfolio
- Board decision and challenge cards

## Questions
- What target and boundary are credible and decision-useful?
- Which sequence minimises emissions and strategic risk?
- How are residual emissions and credits governed?
- Which dashboard indicators trigger review or recalculation?

## Detailed procedure
1. Consolidate the verified baseline, boundaries, significant sources, uncertainty and growth assumptions.
2. Define target type, base year, target year, boundary, metric and treatment of structural changes.
3. Build a business-as-usual pathway and a reduction pathway using quantified initiatives from Activities 6-8.
4. Sequence avoid, reduce, substitute and renewable-energy actions by dependency, cost, risk and abatement.
5. Forecast residual emissions and define when offsets may be considered and what quality rules apply.
6. Set a carbon-credit budget, procurement schedule, approval authority, retirement evidence and claim controls.
7. Define governance, funding gates, supplier/customer engagement and annual inventory/review cadence.
8. Create a board dashboard covering gross emissions, intensity, reductions, data quality, risk, capex and retired credits.
9. Stress-test the strategy against high growth, grid-factor change, project delay and credit-price shock.
10. Present the strategy and respond to Finance, Operations, Procurement, Sustainability and Marketing challenges.

## Required evidence
- 2030 pathway model
- Prioritised initiative portfolio
- Offset and credit policy
- Board dashboard and decision memo

## Acceptance check

The strategy reconciles baseline, pathway and residuals; reduction precedes offsetting; financial and delivery risks are explicit; targets, credits and claims have accountable governance.
