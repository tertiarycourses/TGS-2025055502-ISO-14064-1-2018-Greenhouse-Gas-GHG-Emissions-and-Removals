## Principal source basis

- ISO 14064-1:2018 official catalogue and public sample supplied for the course
- ANSI overview of the 2018 revision
- Supplied ISO 14064 family and standards-catalogue references
- GHG Protocol Corporate Standard
- IPCC Sixth Assessment Report science basis
- Tertiary course page and legacy course assets

The courseware explains and applies the standard; it does not reproduce the copyrighted standard text or substitute for an authorised copy needed for formal conformance work.
